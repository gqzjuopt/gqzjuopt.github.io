About

I am a PhD student in Optical Engineering at the College of Optical Science and Engineering, Zhejiang University. My research focuses on atmospheric turbulence and laser propagation, with particular interest in how atmospheric turbulence affects the propagation characteristics of laser beams, as well as related modeling, simulation, and experimental validation.

Research

My research centers on atmospheric turbulence and laser propagation, including atmospheric turbulence channel modeling, analysis of laser propagation characteristics in turbulent media, and related experimental measurement and validation.
